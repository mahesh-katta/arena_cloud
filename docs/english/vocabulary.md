# Vocabulary

Vocabulary in IBPS prelims is tested through reading-comprehension word questions (closest or opposite meaning), word usage, pairs of words, misspelt words, and the choice of word in fillers, cloze tests and word swaps. This topic gives four lists: words, one-word substitutions, spellings and easily confused words.

The words list was ranked by a script that counted each headword across the two question banks and the cached memory-based papers. Counts are small: no word appears more than 3 times in the meaning questions, so learn meanings broadly rather than chasing 'repeat' words. A word's count adds its appearances as a tested or keyed word in meaning questions to its appearances as a keyed, vocabulary-level answer in fillers, cloze tests and word swaps. Very common words that the real papers sometimes ask (such as safe, watch, form) were left out because learners already know them.

Of the 500 words, 410 come from the bank counts (src "bank") and 90 come from standard bank-exam word lists that the harvest did not find (src "web", count 0). The 250 core words are the top of the count ranking (ties: words tested in meaning questions before filler-only words, then source-list order); 0 of them are web items.

One-word substitutions are not a topic in these banks, so their count only says how often the word appears anywhere in English question text; core picks prefer those found (7 core items are web). Spellings come first from misspelt-word questions (count = times the word was the misspelt answer), then standard traps (0 core items are web). Confusables are marked "bank" when two of the words appear together as options in one bank question (31 core items are web).

All meanings, tips and differences are written in plain words with British spelling. Synonyms and antonyms fit the main sense given; many words have other senses.
